MEP Engineering & Contracting Services

ABHINAV MEP ENGINEERS delivers full-range contracting and engineering services in HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing, Fire-Fighting, Security Systems, BMS & Automation, and related Electro-Mechanical works. We work in conformity with international standards of quality, security and safety.

Our scope covers Engineering, Documentation, Submittals Approval, Shop drawings, Coordinating Drawings, Commissioning, Start-Up, and As-Built — complying with Consultant Specifications, International Standards & Codes, and Safety & Quality Plans. We utilise trained and experienced staff with commitment to contract terms and environmental preservation.

Our Capabilities

New Construction

Complete MEP design, supply, installation and commissioning for new buildings — commercial, residential, pharmaceutical, data centres and industrial facilities — from concept to handover.

Renovations & Retrofits

Upgrade and modernise existing HVAC, electrical, plumbing and fire systems with minimal disruption. We support retrofit, capacity expansion and energy-efficiency improvements.

Project Management

End-to-end project control: scheduling, procurement, site coordination, submittals, quality checks and commissioning — ensuring on-time, on-budget delivery and contract compliance.

Quality Assurance

Strict adherence to specifications, codes and safety plans. Documented testing, balancing and commissioning with as-built documentation for validation and audits.

Our Methodology

1

Planning & Design

We begin with site assessment, load calculations and design development in line with client requirements and consultant specifications. Drawings, submittals and permit support are prepared for approval.

  • Site survey and load analysis
  • Design development and shop drawings
  • Submittal preparation and permit support
2

Procurement & Coordination

Equipment and materials are sourced as per approved submittals. We coordinate with other trades and ensure delivery aligned with the construction schedule and site readiness.

  • Procurement and long-lead tracking
  • Coordination drawings and clash resolution
  • Delivery and storage as per schedule
3

Installation & Construction

Skilled teams install HVAC, electrical, plumbing and fire systems as per approved drawings and codes. Quality checks and safety protocols are followed throughout the installation phase.

  • Mechanical, electrical and plumbing installation
  • Testing and pressure checks
  • Quality control and safety compliance
4

Completion & Handover

Systems are balanced, commissioned and started up. As-built documentation, O&M manuals and training are provided. Final inspections and punch-list closure lead to formal handover.

  • Commissioning, start-up and balancing
  • As-built documentation and O&M manuals
  • Client training and project handover
5

Service & Support

After handover, we provide ongoing service and support to keep your MEP systems running efficiently. This includes preventive maintenance, breakdown support, spare parts and performance optimisation as per contract terms.

  • Preventive and corrective maintenance
  • Breakdown support and spare parts
  • Performance monitoring and optimisation
