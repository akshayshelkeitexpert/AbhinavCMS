Pharmaceutical Warehouse HVAC Project

Location: Pune, Maharashtra. Application: Pharmaceutical Warehouse – Temperature Controlled Storage. Operational Requirement: 24 × 7 Continuous Operation. Temperature Criteria: 23°C NMT (Not More Than 23°C).

Total Air135,000 CFM
Temperature23°C NMT

Project Objective

To design and execute a GMP-oriented HVAC system capable of maintaining a controlled warehouse temperature of ≤23°C under continuous 24-hour operation, ensuring compliance with pharmaceutical storage standards and product stability requirements.

The system was engineered to provide: stable temperature control across the entire warehouse; uniform air distribution without hot or cold pockets; high operational reliability for uninterrupted service; energy-efficient performance suitable for long-duration operation.

HVAC System Design & Engineering Details

Air Handling System

  • 3 Nos. Air Handling Units (AHUs)
  • Capacity: 45,000 CFM each
  • Total Air Quantity: 135,000 CFM
  • Designed for high air circulation to ensure thermal uniformity
  • Continuous duty configuration for 24/7 performance

Cooling System

  • Integrated with LG VRF (Variable Refrigerant Flow) System
  • Zoned temperature control
  • High part-load efficiency
  • Precise refrigerant modulation for stable temperature maintenance

Air Distribution Network

  • GI (Galvanized Iron) ducting as per industrial standards
  • Jet diffusers strategically positioned for long-throw air distribution
  • Uniform airflow coverage across the warehouse floor area

Compliance & Technical Considerations

  • Designed considering pharmaceutical storage norms
  • Temperature maintained at ≤23°C (NMT) under full load conditions
  • Uniform air velocity and air change effectiveness
  • Suitable for temperature-sensitive pharma products
  • System designed for long-term operational stability
  • Optimized insulation to reduce condensation risk

Engineering Outcome

The installed HVAC system ensures:

  • Controlled warehouse environment suitable for pharmaceutical storage
  • Stable temperature performance during continuous operation
  • High reliability with minimal fluctuation
  • Energy-efficient large-volume air handling

This project demonstrates Abhinav MEP Engineers Pvt. Ltd.'s capability in delivering pharma-grade warehouse HVAC solutions, integrating high-capacity AHUs with VRF technology and insulated ducting systems for precise environmental control.
