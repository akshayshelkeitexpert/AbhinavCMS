Project Engineering Overview

Abhinav MEP Engineers Pvt. Ltd. executed a fully integrated industrial-grade MEP and solar infrastructure project at Chakan MIDC, Pune. The project required coordination between industrial utility areas and administrative office spaces, ensuring operational reliability, safety compliance, and energy optimization.

The execution involved mechanical system installation, electrical infrastructure development, fire protection modification, plumbing networks, and renewable energy integration within a defined 8-month schedule.

HVAC Engineering – 160 HP VRV System

System Configuration

  • Supply & Installation of 160 HP VRV System
  • Industrial-grade Daikin Make VRV Technology
  • Zoned cooling configuration for office blocks
  • Inverter-driven compressor technology for part-load efficiency
  • Refrigerant piping network with pressure-tested joints

Engineering Considerations

  • Heat load calculation based on occupancy, lighting, and equipment load
  • Refrigerant pipe sizing based on equivalent length and pressure drop
  • Electrical load coordination with main distribution system
  • Continuous-duty design for industrial office operations

The system was engineered to ensure stable indoor temperature control while maintaining energy-efficient performance under varying operational loads.

Electrical Infrastructure Engineering

Scope of Electrical Works

  • Complete office electrification
  • Main and sub-distribution panel installation
  • Power cable routing and structured wiring
  • Lighting system installation
  • Earthing and safety grounding network

Technical Execution

  • Connected load and demand load calculation
  • Phase load balancing for distribution stability
  • Voltage drop consideration across cable runs
  • Integration with HVAC and fire alarm systems

The electrical system was designed to meet industrial reliability standards with provision for future load expansion.

Plumbing System Execution

  • Industrial and office water supply piping
  • Drainage and sanitary line installation
  • Proper slope and flow velocity consideration
  • Pressure-tested and leak-proof commissioning

The plumbing network was designed for operational durability and maintenance efficiency within the industrial environment.

Fire Alarm & Hydrant Line Modification

Fire Protection Engineering

  • Fire alarm system installation and modification
  • Hydrant line modification to suit revised layout
  • Pipeline routing with proper pressure rating
  • Functional testing and safety compliance checks

Hydraulic considerations and statutory fire norms were integrated to ensure compliance and enhanced safety across the facility.

Solar Power Integration

  • Installation of solar energy system for industrial application
  • Structural mounting and electrical integration
  • Synchronization with main power distribution
  • Designed to reduce grid dependency and operational cost

The solar infrastructure supports long-term sustainability and energy optimization goals of the facility.

Industrial Engineering Challenges Addressed

  • Coordination between industrial production zones and office areas
  • Electrical load integration with HVAC systems
  • Fire line modification without disrupting operations
  • Structured service routing within industrial constraints
  • Integrated MEP and renewable energy execution under single management

Project Outcome

The completed project delivers:

  • Reliable 160 HP VRV-based office cooling system
  • Industrial-grade electrical infrastructure
  • Modified and compliant fire protection system
  • Durable plumbing installation
  • Renewable solar power integration
  • Seamless coordination across all MEP disciplines
