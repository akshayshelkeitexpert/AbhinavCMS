Pharmaceutical Research & Development Centre – Integrated HVAC & MEP Execution

Mumbai, Maharashtra. Project Type: Pharmaceutical R&D Facility. Project Duration: 24 Months. Scope: Complete SITC of HVAC, Electrical & Fire Protection Systems.

Duration24 Months
LocationMumbai, Maharashtra

Project Vision

Pharmaceutical R&D facilities demand precision-engineered environmental control systems to ensure research integrity, product stability, and regulatory readiness.

Abhinav MEP Engineers Pvt. Ltd. delivered a fully integrated HVAC and MEP solution for a state-of-the-art Pharmaceutical Research & Development Centre in Mumbai, ensuring reliable performance, energy efficiency, and compliance-oriented infrastructure.

HVAC Engineering Excellence – 22 Nos. Air Handling Units (AHUs)

  • Supply, Installation, Testing & Commissioning of 22 AHUs of various capacities
  • Designed for laboratory zones, research spaces, and controlled environments
  • Engineered for consistent temperature and airflow distribution
  • Optimized ADP (Apparatus Dew Point) selection for effective humidity control
  • Continuous-duty configuration for mission-critical operations

The HVAC design focused on maintaining stable environmental conditions essential for pharmaceutical research activities.

Precision Air Distribution System

  • GI ducting network with thermal insulation
  • Engineered static pressure and airflow balancing
  • Condensation prevention through proper insulation thickness
  • Uniform air distribution across multiple functional zones

The air distribution layout was carefully planned to ensure performance efficiency and long-term durability.

Chilled Water Piping Infrastructure

  • Complete chilled water piping network
  • Thermal insulation to minimize energy loss
  • Pressure-tested installation
  • Hydraulically balanced system for uniform cooling delivery

The chilled water system ensures consistent cooling performance across all research and administrative areas.

VRF System for Office & Administrative Areas

  • SITC of LG Make VRF System
  • Energy-efficient inverter technology
  • Zoned cooling control
  • Silent and reliable performance
  • Integrated with overall HVAC infrastructure

This solution ensures optimal comfort for office spaces while maintaining operational efficiency.

Electrical & Fire Protection Infrastructure

Complete Building Electrification

  • HVAC power supply & control wiring
  • Distribution panels & load management
  • Earthing & safety systems
  • Coordinated MEP integration

The electrical system was engineered for reliability, safety, and seamless operational performance.

Fire Hydrant & Sprinkler System

  • Complete fire hydrant network
  • Automatic sprinkler system installation
  • Hydraulic calculations & pump selection
  • Wet riser distribution system
  • Designed as per statutory fire safety norms

Ensuring life safety and compliance was a core component of the project execution strategy.
