Residential & Commercial High-Rise MEP Project

Building Configuration: 22 Floors + Basement Levels. Scope: Complete MEP Design, Engineering & Execution.

Building 22 Floors + Basement
Location Pune, Maharashtra

Technical Project Overview

This project involved the comprehensive design, load analysis, system engineering, and execution of integrated MEP infrastructure for a 22-storey mixed-use residential and commercial tower in Pune.

The engineering scope included HVAC systems, electrical infrastructure, plumbing networks, fire fighting systems, basement ventilation, and vertical service coordination.

Mechanical System Engineering (HVAC & Ventilation)

HVAC Load Assessment & Design

  • Detailed heat load calculations considering occupancy, lighting, equipment loads, glass façade exposure, and Pune climatic conditions
  • Floor-wise TR estimation and zoning strategy
  • Diversified load factor application for mixed-use occupancy
  • Equipment capacity selection based on peak demand and diversity factor

Ventilation & Pressurization Systems

  • Basement ventilation system designed as per air changes per hour (ACH) requirement
  • Car park exhaust and fresh air system sizing based on CFM calculations
  • Staircase pressurization system engineered to maintain required positive pressure
  • Static pressure calculations and duct sizing as per friction loss methodology

System Capacities (Indicative Engineering Scale)

  • Multi-floor HVAC zoning strategy
  • High-capacity air handling and ventilation units
  • Vertical duct risers with calculated pressure drops
  • Balanced airflow distribution across 22 levels

Electrical Engineering & Power Infrastructure

Electrical Load Calculation & Distribution Design

  • Connected load calculation (residential + commercial)
  • Demand load assessment with diversity factor
  • Transformer sizing based on maximum demand
  • HT/LT panel engineering and selection

Infrastructure Executed

  • Main LT panels and sub-distribution panels
  • Rising mains and bus duct systems
  • DG backup system sizing based on essential load
  • Lightning protection and earthing system design
  • Fire alarm system infrastructure

Load balancing and phase distribution were engineered to ensure voltage stability across vertical risers.

Plumbing & Fire Fighting Engineering

Plumbing System Design

  • Domestic water demand calculation per occupancy standards
  • Pump head calculation for 22-floor vertical lift
  • Hydro-pneumatic system design
  • Pipe sizing based on flow velocity and pressure loss

Fire Fighting System

  • Hydrant and sprinkler system hydraulic calculations
  • Fire pump capacity selection
  • Terrace tank and underground tank capacity sizing
  • Wet riser and landing valve distribution

All systems were designed to comply with statutory norms and high-rise safety requirements.

High-Rise Engineering Challenges Addressed

  • Vertical service shaft optimization
  • Pressure zoning for plumbing networks
  • Voltage drop management in tall structures
  • Basement smoke extraction design
  • Integrated coordination between mechanical, electrical & plumbing systems
  • Structural and architectural integration
