Industrial Process HVAC & Utility Engineering Project

Fertilizers Manufacturing Facility – Supa MIDC, Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra. Client Industry: Fertilizers Manufacturing. Scope: Process HVAC, Chilled Water System, VRF System, Industrial Ventilation & Allied Electrical Works. Executed By: Abhinav MEP Engineers Pvt. Ltd.

Client IndustryFertilizers Manufacturing
LocationSupa MIDC, Ahilyanagar, Maharashtra

Project Engineering Overview

Abhinav MEP Engineers Pvt. Ltd. executed a comprehensive process-oriented industrial HVAC and utility infrastructure project for a fertilizers manufacturing facility at Supa MIDC.

The project required integration of comfort HVAC for administrative areas, process cooling for manufacturing zones, and engineered ventilation systems to manage heat loads, airborne particulates, and operational emissions typical in fertilizer production environments.

Design parameters were developed considering: high internal heat loads from process equipment; continuous plant operation; corrosive industrial environment; air change requirements as per industrial ventilation norms; energy efficiency and operational reliability.

Detailed Scope of Work

1. Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF) System – Administrative & Controlled Zones

Supply and installation of Carrier Make VRF System for office and utility blocks.

Technical Execution:

  • Heat load calculation based on occupancy, lighting, and equipment load
  • Refrigerant piping design with oil return calculations
  • Pressure testing and nitrogen flushing
  • Vacuum dehydration below recommended micron levels
  • Individual zone control with inverter-driven compressors
  • BMS-ready integration provision

The VRF system ensures modular capacity control, part-load efficiency, and optimized power consumption for non-process areas.

2. Industrial Chilled Water System – Process Cooling Network

A dedicated chilled water system was engineered to serve process areas and AHUs.

Engineering Features:

  • Chilled water piping layout based on hydraulic flow calculations
  • Flow balancing using control valves
  • Thermal insulation to prevent condensation and heat gain
  • Pump head calculations and selection
  • Provision for maintenance isolation valves
  • Expansion and air venting arrangements

The system was designed to ensure stable supply temperature and uniform cooling distribution across process zones.

3. Air Handling Units (AHUs) – Process Air Management

Installation of industrial-grade AHUs designed for continuous duty operation.

Technical Highlights:

  • High static pressure blowers
  • Industrial filtration stages
  • Airflow design based on required Air Changes Per Hour (ACH)
  • Duct static pressure calculations
  • Uniform air distribution for heat dissipation
  • Corrosion-resistant components suitable for fertilizer environments

AHUs were integrated with the chilled water network to provide controlled temperature and air circulation across manufacturing sections.

4. Industrial Ventilation & Air Movement Systems

Ventilation was a critical component due to: process heat generation; dust and particulate exposure; emission management; worker safety requirements.

Scope Included:

  • Fresh air supply systems
  • Exhaust air systems
  • Duct routing optimized for minimum pressure loss
  • ACH calculations as per industrial standards
  • Mechanical ventilation units for continuous air extraction

The ventilation system ensures dilution of airborne contaminants and maintenance of safe working conditions.

5. Allied Electrical Infrastructure for HVAC & Utilities

Complete electrical backbone for HVAC and ventilation systems:

  • Power distribution panels
  • Control wiring for VRF and AHUs
  • Cable tray routing and structured cabling
  • Earthing and safety compliance
  • Load distribution planning

Electrical systems were engineered for reliability under continuous industrial operation.

Engineering & Execution Methodology

  • Site survey and load analysis
  • HVAC design calculations (CFM, TR, pump head, static pressure)
  • Hydraulic balancing of chilled water network
  • Pressure testing and commissioning protocols
  • Electrical load planning and system integration
  • Final performance testing and commissioning

Operational Outcomes

  • Stable process cooling performance
  • Controlled ambient temperature in production zones
  • Improved ventilation and air quality
  • Energy-efficient cooling in administrative areas
  • Reliable electrical integration
  • Enhanced plant operational stability
