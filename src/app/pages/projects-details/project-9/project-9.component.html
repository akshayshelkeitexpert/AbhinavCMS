Pharmaceutical Cleanroom HVAC Project – Pithampur, Madhya Pradesh

Project Type: Pharmaceutical Manufacturing Facility – Cleanroom HVAC & BMS Integration. Location: Pithampur, Madhya Pradesh. Project Duration: 22 Months. Scope: Design, Supply, Installation, Testing & Commissioning of Complete Cleanroom HVAC & Electrical Systems.

Duration22 Months
LocationPithampur, Madhya Pradesh

Project Objective

To engineer and execute a GMP-compliant cleanroom HVAC system capable of maintaining controlled environmental conditions for pharmaceutical manufacturing operations.

The project required: precise temperature and airflow control; pressure cascade management between clean zones; reliable 24/7 operational stability; centralized monitoring through BMS integration; compliance-ready infrastructure for validation and audits.

Cleanroom HVAC System Configuration

1. Air Handling Units (AHUs)

  • 24 Nos. AHUs of various capacities
  • Dedicated units for production areas, airlocks, and utility zones
  • Designed for controlled air changes per hour (ACH)
  • Suitable for classified cleanroom environments
  • Configured for pressure differential control

The AHUs were engineered to maintain uniform airflow distribution and support cleanroom pressure hierarchy.

2. DX Cooling Systems

  • Supply & Installation of Voltas Make DX Units
  • Direct expansion systems for temperature-sensitive zones
  • Fast thermal response for process load variations
  • Energy-efficient and reliable performance

3. VRF System Integration

  • 230 HP VRF System
  • Supply & Installation of Voltas Make VRF System
  • Zoned cooling for controlled production areas
  • Stable refrigerant modulation for tight temperature control
  • Suitable for multi-area pharmaceutical layouts

4. Chilled Water Piping Network

  • Complete chilled water piping system installation
  • Thermal insulation to prevent condensation and thermal loss
  • Proper slope and routing for maintenance accessibility
  • Pressure-tested and hydraulically balanced network

The piping system was designed to ensure consistent chilled water flow and temperature stability across AHU coils.

Electrical & BMS Integration

A fully integrated HVAC electrical system was executed including: control panels for AHUs and VRF systems; power distribution and safety systems; integration with Building Management System (BMS).

The BMS enables:

  • Real-time monitoring of temperature and airflow
  • Pressure differential monitoring between clean zones
  • Alarm logging and system diagnostics
  • Operational trend analysis for validation support

Cleanroom Engineering Considerations

  • Designed to support cleanroom classifications (as per pharma requirements)
  • Air change rates configured for process zones
  • Pressure cascade planning between production areas
  • Controlled supply and return air balancing
  • Continuous-duty operation for uninterrupted manufacturing

Project Outcome

The completed cleanroom HVAC system provides:

  • Controlled and stable manufacturing environments
  • Reliable temperature and airflow management
  • Pressure-controlled cleanroom zoning
  • Centralized system monitoring via BMS
  • Infrastructure suitable for GMP audits and validation

This project demonstrates Abhinav MEP Engineers Pvt. Ltd.'s capability in delivering pharmaceutical cleanroom HVAC systems, integrating AHUs, DX units, VRF systems, chilled water networks, and advanced control systems for regulated manufacturing facilities.
