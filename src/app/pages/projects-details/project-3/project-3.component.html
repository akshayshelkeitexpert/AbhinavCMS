Project Vision

A luxury residence demands more than just infrastructure — it requires precision engineering, silent performance, and seamless integration.

Abhinav MEP Engineers Pvt. Ltd. delivered a fully integrated premium MEP solution for this exclusive residential bungalow in Pune, ensuring world-class comfort, safety, and operational efficiency.

Every system was designed to enhance lifestyle quality while maintaining architectural elegance and long-term reliability.

Premium HVAC Solution – 140 HP Advanced VRF Air Conditioning System

The residence is equipped with a high-performance 140 HP VRF system from Toshiba, globally recognized for innovation and reliability.

Key Features:

  • Individual zoned climate control for personalized comfort
  • Energy-efficient inverter compressor technology
  • Ultra-quiet indoor units for disturbance-free living
  • Smart load management for optimized power consumption
  • Aesthetic indoor unit integration with premium interiors

The VRF system ensures consistent cooling performance across the entire bungalow while maintaining energy efficiency and operational silence.

Complete Electrical Engineering

A sophisticated home requires a robust and safe electrical backbone. The project included:

  • Structured internal & external electrical wiring
  • Premium distribution panels
  • Optimized load balancing and circuit design
  • Advanced safety and earthing systems
  • Integrated lighting infrastructure

All installations were executed with precision planning, ensuring safety, reliability, and future scalability.

Precision Plumbing Infrastructure

The plumbing system was engineered to match the luxury standard of the residence:

  • High-quality water supply piping network
  • Efficient drainage and sanitary systems
  • Pressure-tested installation for leak-proof performance
  • Smart routing for ease of maintenance
  • Premium-grade fittings and execution standards

The result is a durable, maintenance-friendly, and high-performance plumbing infrastructure.
