About ABHINAV MEP ENGINEERS

ABHINAV MEP ENGINEERS is a pan-India Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing contracting company specialising in design, build, construct, supply, install, service, upgrade, and maintenance of Electro-Mechanical Systems & Networks, Utilities and Equipment. We target and serve clients across India.

Our scope covers Engineering, Documentation, Submittals Approval, Shop drawings, Coordinating Drawings, Commissioning, Start-Up, and As-Built in accordance with Contract Programs, Project Time Schedules — complying with Consultant Specifications, International Standards & Codes, Safety & Quality Plans. We work with the highest efficiency and in accordance with the latest technologies in conformity with international standards of quality, security and safety. We utilise trained and experienced staff for the implementation and delivery of projects across India, with commitment to contract terms, conditions, and preservation to the environment.

Background

ABHINAV MEP ENGINEERS is a major pan-India player in Electro-mechanical fields with a good track record for Supplies and Turnkey Projects, serving clients across India — from metros to tier-II and tier-III cities. Our primary purpose is to complete the supply, install, service and upgrade chains in the Electro-Mechanical fields. With our staff experience and technical know-how, we have become a one-stop shop for our clients' total Electro-Mechanical needs nationwide.

ABHINAV MEP ENGINEERS enjoys a strong pan-India presence in: Trading, Contracting, Services, Maintenance, Manufacturing, and Development. This is the result of high efficiency and full autonomy in Planning & Coordination, Engineering, Quality Control, and After-Sales.
