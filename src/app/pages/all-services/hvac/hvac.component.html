HVAC Systems & Services

Our HVAC scope of services encompasses the following systems and solutions:

Chilled Water Systems – Centralized cooling plants including chillers, primary/secondary pumping, piping networks, and terminal integrations.

VRV/VRF Systems – Design, supply, installation, and commissioning of variable refrigerant flow systems for commercial and industrial applications.

Clean Room HVAC Systems – Precision-controlled environments with temperature, humidity, and particulate management as per required ISO standards.

Cold Storage Solutions – Refrigeration systems for temperature-controlled storage facilities with optimized energy performance.

Ducting & Insulation Works – Fabrication and installation of GI/PI ducts with thermal and acoustic insulation as per SMACNA standards.

FCU & AHU Systems – Installation, testing, and commissioning of Fan Coil Units and Air Handling Units with integrated control systems.

Air Balancing & Commissioning – TAB (Testing, Adjusting & Balancing) services to ensure optimal airflow distribution and system performance.

Ventilation Systems – Fresh air supply, exhaust air systems, and industrial ventilation solutions.

Kitchen Exhaust & Scrubber Systems – Commercial kitchen ventilation including hoods, ducting, blowers, and pollution control systems.

Our HVAC Capabilities

Chilled Water System

Design and installation of chilled water systems for large buildings and facilities. We provide centralized cooling plants with chillers, primary/secondary pumping, piping networks, and terminal integrations for commercial and industrial applications.

VRV/VRF System

Variable refrigerant volume and flow systems for efficient climate control. We design, supply, install and commission VRV/VRF systems for zoned cooling in offices, retail, and industrial spaces with optimized energy performance.

Clean Room

Precision-controlled clean room HVAC with temperature, humidity and particulate management as per ISO standards. We design and install AHUs, HEPA filtration, pressure cascade and validation support for pharma, electronics and healthcare.

Air Balancing

Ventilation systems, air balancing and kitchen exhaust. We provide TAB (Testing, Adjusting & Balancing) services to ensure optimal airflow distribution, static pressure and system performance as per design specifications.

Ducting

Fabrication and installation of GI/PIR ducting with thermal and acoustic insulation as per SMACNA standards. We provide layout, sizing, supports and leak testing for supply, return and exhaust networks in buildings.

FCU & AHU

Supply, installation, testing and commissioning of Fan Coil Units and Air Handling Units with integrated controls. We size and select equipment for chilled water and DX applications in commercial and industrial buildings.

Cold Storages

Refrigeration and cold storage solutions for warehouses, pharma and food & beverage. We design and install temperature-controlled storage with optimized energy performance and compliance for sensitive products.

Evaporative Cooling

Packaged, ducted and split units including evaporative cooling. We provide single-stage and two-stage evaporative systems for direct and indirect cooling applications to reduce overall energy consumption.
