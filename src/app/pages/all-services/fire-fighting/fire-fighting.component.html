Fire-Fighting & Fire Safety Systems

ABHINAV MEP ENGINEERS delivers fire-fighting and fire safety systems including sprinkler installation and modification, fire alarm systems, FACP programming, testing and commissioning. We ensure compliance with Civil Defence approvals and EHS certification.

Our scope includes emergency and exit lighting systems, fire suppression systems, and full documentation for regulatory compliance. We design and execute fire protection systems for commercial, industrial, pharmaceutical, and residential projects across India, adhering to national and international codes. Our team provides end-to-end support from design and hydraulic calculations to installation, testing, and as-built documentation.

We work with approved equipment and follow statutory requirements for high-rise buildings, warehouses, data centres, clean rooms, and manufacturing facilities.

Our Fire-Fighting Capabilities

Sprinkler Systems

Design, supply, installation and modification of fire sprinkler systems including wet-pipe, dry-pipe, and pre-action systems. We carry out hydraulic calculations, pipe sizing, and pump selection as per NFPA and local norms. Our scope covers sprinkler heads, risers, valves, and integration with fire pumps and water supply.

Fire Alarm

Fire alarm system installation and modification with FACP (Fire Alarm Control Panel) programming, addressable and conventional detection, manual call points, and integration with sprinkler flow switches and other building systems. We provide commissioning and handover documentation for approvals.

Emergency / Exit Lighting

Emergency and exit lighting systems for safe egress in compliance with building codes. We install maintained and non-maintained luminaires, central battery systems, and ensure adequate illumination along escape routes and at exit signs for evacuation during power failure.

Civil Defence & EHS

Support for Civil Defence approvals and EHS certification. We prepare submittals, as-built drawings, and test reports required for NOC and occupancy. Our documentation meets regulatory and client EHS requirements for fire safety compliance.

Fire Suppression

Fire suppression system design and installation including gas-based suppression (FM-200, CO2, etc.) for critical areas such as server rooms and control panels. We provide detection, cylinder storage, piping, nozzles, and integration with building automation and fire alarm.

Testing & Commissioning

FACP programming, device addressing, loop testing, and full commissioning of fire alarm and sprinkler systems. We perform flow tests, pump tests, and battery tests, and hand over operation manuals and as-built documentation to the client.
